      Remove hidden data

      Photos and documents often carry hidden details: where a photo was taken, the camera and its serial number, dates, and names. See what’s in your files, then remove it before you share them.

      What gets removed

      Location, camera and lens details, serial numbers, dates, owner and author names, editing software, comments, hidden thumbnails, and AI image settings. In PDFs: author, title, software and dates.

      JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF are cleaned without re-saving the picture, so there’s no quality loss. HEIC and other formats are converted to JPEG (or PNG if they have transparency).
